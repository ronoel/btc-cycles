**Health check (monthly):** confirm the GitHub Action is green (repo → Actions → "Update on-chain data"), that `data.json`'s `updated` field is recent, and that **`data.json`'s `stale` array is empty** — it lists any source that failed and kept its previous value. `scripts/build_data.py` never fails the build on a bad source, so a silently degraded feed shows up there and nowhere else.

**Data-pipeline facts worth not rediscovering:**
- Everything is **keyless**. `BGEOMETRICS_TOKEN` only lifts bitcoin-data.com's anonymous rate limit (**10 requests/hour** — easy to trip when testing locally) and may widen the history window.
- **The on-chain list is now 8 of those 10 requests**, so retries — not the list — are what trips the limit. `fetch_onchain` passes `tries=2` for exactly that reason. Adding a ninth series without setting `BGEOMETRICS_TOKEN` is asking for a half-stale `data.json`. Practical note for local work: once you hit the 429 the whole hour is gone, so pull a series **once** and keep the JSON on disk instead of re-fetching while iterating.
- **BGeometrics endpoint quirks, learned the hard way (Aug 19, 2026):** the `/{last}` suffix returns an **empty 200** for some series (`sth-mvrv/3`, `sth-realized-price/3`) while working for others — fetch the bare path and slice. Series names are not systematic: it is `realized-price-sth`, not `sth-realized-price`. `exchange-reserve-btc` now returns **HTTP 403 (subscription)**, which is why the `resv` row stays manual. Free and verified working, and the next candidate to wire up: **`hashribbons`**, which returns `{sma_30, sma_60, hashribbons:"Down"}` — see §2b before scoring on it. The full endpoint list is at `https://api.bitcoin-data.com/v3/api-docs` (688 paths).
- **FRED sends no CORS header**, so its series can never be fetched from the page; they must come through the Action. Verified 2026-08-06.
- The **SoSoValue ETF endpoint is undocumented** (`POST api.sosovalue.xyz/openapi/v2/etf/historicalInflowChart`, body `{"type":"us-btc-spot"}`). It works keyless and happens to echo any `Origin`, but it is an internal endpoint and may change without notice — which is why it is routed through the Action and why its failure only marks `etf` stale.
- **`mvrv` and `mvrv-zscore` are different endpoints.** The ratio is ~1.24 today; the Z-score ~0.39. Both are published. Do not collapse them again — that bug lived in the report for its whole life until Aug 2026.
- `history.json` (~60KB) holds the daily on-chain series and exists **only** for the retro-calibration section. If it is missing or malformed the section hides itself and nothing else breaks.
- The free history window is exactly **1,461 days**, so the calibration can reach the Nov 2022 bottom and not the Dec 2018 one. If the token ever widens that window, `calibTrace()` will pick it up automatically — check whether cycle 2 becomes scoreable.
- **Wiring a new on-chain series takes FOUR edits, not two**, and the failure mode is silent. `scripts/build_data.py` (the `ONCHAIN` list) → `data.json`/`history.json` → **`fetchOnChainData()`'s `onChainData` object in `index.html`, which is a whitelist** → the row's `RULE` + `put()` call. Skip the third and the row reads `null`, falls back to its static research text, loses its `LIVE` badge, and reports no error at all. That happened to `sth_mvrv` on Aug 19, 2026 and was caught only by diffing the rendered cell against the value the rule should have produced.
- **`history.json` must carry every series in `CORE`.** `coreAt()` returns `null` if any CORE rule reads null, so a missing series does not degrade the calibration — it empties the trace and the whole section hides itself. When adding a signal to `CORE`, run `scripts/build_data.py` and confirm the new key is in `history.json` **before** pushing.

## 2b. Signal & trigger conventions

Rules that keep the checklist honest over time. **Never retro-edit a threshold because you dislike the reading it just produced** — change the spec prospectively and record it here.

This section holds *conventions*. The derivations, tested counter-arguments and data
limitations behind each conclusion go in [`ANALYSIS-LOG.md`](ANALYSIS-LOG.md); add an
entry there whenever a research pass changes a threshold, a weight or the thesis.

**Scoring structure (Aug 2026).** The headline is an **ordinal**, not a percentage: 15 signals in four families (`BS_FAM` — Valuation / Capitulation / Supply & flows / Macro), a family lights on a **strict majority** of active signals (PARTIAL counts a half), and lit-family count maps to TOO EARLY / APPROACHING / BOTTOM ZONE / CONFIRMED. Rules to preserve:
- The majority test must stay **strict (`> n/2`)**. With `>=`, a family of all-PARTIAL signals sums to exactly `n/2` and lights with nothing active — this shipped briefly and rendered "BOTTOM ZONE" on 2 of 15 active signals.
- **Both** the expert-weighted and equal-weighted scores are published, permanently. They currently agree to within ~3 points and agreed to within 1 at the 2022 bottom. If a change ever makes them diverge materially, that divergence is a finding to investigate, not a number to pick between.
- `BS_W` must still sum to **100**, now across **15** keys.
- **Every computable threshold lives in the `RULE` object** and nowhere else, because the live checklist and the retro-calibration both call it. Never inline a threshold at a call site — the whole value of the calibration is that both eras are scored by identical code.
- **`CORE` membership is decided on data quality, before looking at what it does to the headline.** The two tests are: a clean daily series covering *both* eras, and a threshold that was fixed *before* the series was in hand. `sthmvrv` joined on Aug 19, 2026 under exactly that rule — its thresholds were specified Jul 24, a month before the live series was found. Conditioning membership on whether a signal flatters the 2022 reading is how a calibration quietly becomes a fit. Whatever number the expanded subset prints at the 2022 bottom is the number that gets published, including if it is below 100%.
- **A manual row is allowed to become LIVE, but not to change definition on the way.** When wiring one up, the rule must be the threshold already published for it. If the only free series measures something adjacent (e.g. `exchange-supply-ratio` in place of the paywalled exchange reserve), that is a **spec change dressed as a data upgrade** — leave the row manual and say the data is missing, which is what `resv` now does.
- **`sthmvrv`'s ≤0.80 threshold is under prospective review, and was deliberately NOT changed on Aug 19, 2026.** Getting the series showed the Nov 2022 bottom read 0.84, the summer-2022 bear-rally high read 0.831 (*lower* than the bottom), and the series sat ≤0.80 for 32 days in Feb–Mar 2026 without marking anything. So the level does not mark bottoms. It was left alone because loosening it to ≤0.85 in that same pass would have restored the 100% calibration headline — the textbook motivated edit. If a future pass revises it, the revision is prospective, the derivation goes in `ANALYSIS-LOG.md`, and **the calibration headline moves with it**, so `README.md`, `cal_sum`, `th_p` and `og-card.html` all need updating together.
- **Do not score `hashribbons` on a naive `sma_30 > sma_60`.** The classic signal is stateful — a cross *after* a capitulation — and a bare crossover reads ACTIVE through any ordinary bull market. Before wiring the free BGeometrics endpoint into `RULE`, check what its `hashribbons` state field said in **Jan 2026** (the known misfire, followed by a further 20%+ decline) and at the **Nov 2022 bottom**. Until then the row is manual and the measured 30d/60d averages are quoted in its text.
- **A new checklist row needs three map entries, not one:** `BS_W` (and rebalance to 100), `BS_FAM`, and `BS_SPEED`. A missing `BS_SPEED` key fails silently — the row simply renders with no latency tag.
- **Family weight shares are derived, never written down.** Each family card shows its share of the expert-weighted score computed from `BS_W` at render time. Do not hardcode those percentages into any tooltip or note; if a weight changes the cards follow automatically and a hardcoded copy would not.

**Response-latency tags (Aug 2026, display only).** `BS_SPEED` tags each row FAST (days: funding, ETF flows, Coinbase premium, drawdown, 200W-MA cross) / MEDIUM (weeks: SOPR, STH-MVRV, macro composite) / SLOW (months: Realized Price, NUPL, MVRV-Z, Puell, Hash Ribbons, LTH supply, exchange reserves). It affects **no score**. The reason it exists is the 7-slow / 3-medium / 5-fast split: fast rows improving while slow valuation rows stay dark is the expected behaviour of a mostly-slow instrument mid-turn, not a signal conflict. If a row's data source changes cadence, re-check its tag.

**Event-contaminated readings (formalised Aug 12, 2026, generalising the hash-row precedent).** When a manual row's series is (a) moved by an identifiable non-economic event (e.g. the Coldcard-exploit wallet migration resetting coin age and pushing custody flows onto exchanges) or (b) materially provider-divergent, the row scores **PARTIAL** — it is not read in either direction. Re-read it once the event washes out. First applications: `hash` (Aug 6), `lth` + `resv` (Aug 12). This is a reading convention, not a threshold change — the thresholds stay as specified.

**Latched vs live signals.** Some checklist rows are state ("is it true right now"), others are events ("has it happened this cycle"). The 200W MA row is an *event*: once a weekly close prints below the line, the signal has fired for the cycle even if price reclaims it. The row keeps rendering LIVE state — the latch lives in its tooltip and must be kept there.
- Latched so far in cycle 4: **200W MA — week of Jun 22–28, 2026 closed $59,577 vs a $62,414 200W MA** (−4.5%; first since 2022; reclaimed after).
  Figures are Binance-derived, matching what the page computes — prefer those over news-reported values from other indices.
- **Cycle-phase anchoring:** the capitulation phase is dated from the *flush*, not the low, matching C2 (2018-06-22) and C3 (2022-06-13). C4 is anchored on the Jun 22–28, 2026 breakdown week. Whenever `PHASES.c4` boundaries move, re-check that each `d` still equals its date span and that the ~140–150-day capitulation estimate still lands inside the stated window.

## 5. Standing analytical cautions

Recorded so a future pass does not have to rediscover them, and does not quietly drop them.
